# Welcome to Pixl

^ So here's the deal. Pixl is a YSWS (you ship, we ship) from Hack Club, and the whole thing is wrapped in a story. Origin used to be a huge digital civilization until something called the Great Static tore it apart into floating islands. The people who lived there found their way to Hack Clubbers, and together you're rebuilding it under a new name: Pixl.

But you don't actually need to care about any of that to play. Strip away the story and Pixl is really just this: build real stuff, ship it, get real rewards. NPCs across the game world hand out trials, which are basically project prompts with a bit of flavor text attached. You pick one, build it (or build something totally different that you thought of yourself, an Invention of your own), submit it, and you get pixels plus whatever prize is attached.

You explore a retro 2D world with your own character, run into NPCs, take on quests, and every hour you spend building something real adds up. It's not a race against other people either. Whatever you ship is yours to keep, no matter how the rest of the community is doing.

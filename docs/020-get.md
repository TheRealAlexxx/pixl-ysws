# What do you actually get

Every hour of shipped work pays out in pixels. It starts at {{basePx}} an hour and climbs as you ship more and more ambitious things, up to {{maxPx}} an hour. [Restoration Energy and levels](/docs/energy/) explains exactly how. Most trials have their own prize attached, things like a custom domain, an Apple Developer account, a Flipper Zero, robux, PCB manufacturing budget, whatever fits the quest. When a trial ship is approved you choose: take the prize, or take the pixels that project earned. It's one or the other, so if the prize isn't your thing, take the pixels and buy what you actually want from the shop. Either way the ship banks {{trialBonusRe}} bonus Restoration Energy on top, so trials always move your level and the vault further than the same hours built solo.

Pixels themselves go into the in game shop, where you can spend them on stickers all the way up to bigger stuff like a MacBook Air. The shop catalog and prices show up once you're in game.

Outside of pixels and prizes you also get access to free hosting, free domains, AI credits, a CDN, and starter guides to get you going, more on all of that below.

If you get stuck at any point, search for it first. Someone in #pixl has almost definitely run into the same wall you're stuck on. If you really can't find an answer, just ask.
